Guide & Changelog

Made by Avalon your favorite Guild from Blessed Divider.

  • 50 Stamina & 5 Tools each are added from Daily Quest after every Reset.
  • Daily 2 Hour Speedup: already included in calculations.
  • Save 34hr Bed EXP: is for the end of Season, where you can save a Max of 36hr.
  • Cart Production & Statues/Day: simplified so that every new Statue unlock will boost it by 0.5%. You can also just completely disable Daily Statue for your Cart production.
  • Projected Score Limit Levels: are the Average level of that category.
  • Rolla: completely ignored in the Calculation.
  • Nexus Time: Time is synced to Nexus 16 use [Custom End Date] for your own server.
  • Update to Now: from the last time saved, adds Cart Resources to saved, adds x Statues/Tools after every daily Reset. If [Spend Stamina on Ore] is checked, this will also add ore to your saved Resources.
